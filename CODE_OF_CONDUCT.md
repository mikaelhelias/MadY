# Code of conduct

MadY is used and improved by people with different backgrounds, fields and levels of experience.
Everyone taking part in this project — in issues, pull requests and any other project space — is
expected to help keep it welcoming and useful.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, including when you disagree.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding.
- Keep feedback about the work, specific and constructive.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning remarks about anyone, for any reason.
- Personal attacks, threats, or deliberate intimidation.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **madygrapher@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately. The
maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and other contributions, and may block anyone
who does not follow this code.
